Key Rules to Remember

1 Only transitive verbs (verbs that take an object) can be converted to passive voice.
2 The object of the active sentence becomes the subject of the passive sentence.
3 The verb always changes to auxiliary + V3 (past participle) in passive voice.
4 Object pronouns change: me → I, him → he, her → she, them → they, us → we.
5 Present Perfect Continuous, Past Perfect Continuous, and Future Perfect Continuous are generally not used in passive voice.
6 The "by + agent" phrase is optional — it is omitted when the doer is unknown or unimportant.
